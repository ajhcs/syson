Source ownership is explicit after this amendment is reviewed and landed; plan assignment alone grants no lease. Removing only those additions must reproduce the complete earlier contract, all 790 normalized unit records including unknown fields, and all 1,580 original readiness nodes and edges. Static consistency remains distinct from behavioral acceptance.

The next connected product gate remains create/import, edit, save, reload, close/reopen and backend restart with intact semantic, representation, identity, reference and migration state. These prerequisite landings do not accept that journey, native SysON parity or a release. No owner hold or human contributor reservation is released.
